Hi! Welcome to OpenLumara. This program lets you talk to your AI and also lets your AI perform tasks on your behalf. Such a program is also known as a "harness". I (Rose22) wrote these docs by hand, because AI tends to write a whole lot of words for something that can be explained in very few.

This program is intended for use by everyday people, on a variety of hardware, from laptops to low-end desktop PC's to phones, but also on more high-end hardware. I designed it in such a way that everything about it makes local AI more pleasant to use, especially for everyday, non-tech people. It's a harness for life, for managing your calendar, your tasks, and so on, in a way where you can be confident that what you store into it won't be leaked through things like prompt injection attacks.

It also has a robust coding toolset, for the people who want to use it for coding, in the form of the coder module. I personally use it every day to help work on modules for openlumara and sometimes to find and fix bugs in the main codebase, so it's a proven toolset that works well.

You can talk to your AI through telegram and discord, or use the WebUI. I recommend the WebUI, because it's completely self hosted and doesn't go through any companies' chat networks, so your data stays fully private. You can also install third-party channels that other people have made.
